Investigate and fix a gap in TeamAI's worktree lifecycle: commits made directly in a                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
task's worktree can end up permanently unpushed, with nothing in the orchestrator ever                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
detecting or surfacing this.

Observed scenario: a task's pipeline completed normally and reached pr-open (its PR was                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
opened and merged/pending as expected). Sometime after that, a session ran directly in                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
the task's worktree — outside the normal implement/qa-review phase machinery, no                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
corresponding phase transition was logged — and made a commit there. That commit was                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
never pushed to the task's branch. It sat undetected in the local worktree for days,                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
discovered only by manually comparing the worktree's branch HEAD against its                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
remote-tracking branch and noticing they'd diverged. Nothing in the running system                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
flagged the discrepancy: not the PR, not the task's status, not any subsequent pipeline                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
run.

This means: any commit made in a task's worktree outside the exact moment the                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
orchestrator's own push step runs (in runCreatePR / runMerge, or wherever                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         
commitArtifactsToWorktree and the git-push logic live) is silently at risk of never                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
reaching the remote — there's no reconciliation step that checks "does this worktree's                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
branch have commits origin doesn't have" at any point in the lifecycle: not before the                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
worktree is reused for a retry, not before it's torn down, not on a periodic health                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
check, nowhere.

Please:
1. Find every place a worktree gets reused, reset, or deleted (retry flows, wakeup                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
   resume, cleanup after integration, stale-worktree recovery) and check whether any of                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
   them would currently discard or orphan unpushed local commits without warning.
2. Add a safeguard: before a worktree's branch is torn down, reset, or considered                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
   "done," check `git log origin/<branch>..HEAD` (or equivalent) for that worktree. If                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
   it's non-empty, either push automatically or, at minimum, log a loud warning /                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
   surface it on the task so a human notices instead of silently losing the commit's                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
   reachability from the remote (the commit itself stays in the local object store and                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
   reflog, but nothing will ever push it once the worktree is gone).
3. Cover this with a test: a worktree with a local-only commit ahead of its                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
   remote-tracking branch, at the point where the orchestrator would normally clean up                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
   or reuse that worktree — assert the unpushed commit is either pushed or explicitly                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
   flagged, never silently dropped.

This is a distinct issue from any existing wakeup/parallel-group cherry-pick handling —                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
this can happen to any commit made in a worktree at any time, not just ones tied to a                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
scheduled wakeup.
